# Web Space Program: playtest guide

Thank you for testing. This is an early build of a browser spaceflight game in the spirit of the
classic rocket-building sandbox you already know: build a rocket, fly it, reach orbit, go to the Moon.
The solar system is the real one, scaled down to one tenth by default.

We want your honest reaction as an experienced player. You can't hurt anything. If something breaks,
that is useful too.

## Before you start

- **Browser.** Use desktop Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a laptop or desktop with a mouse. Phones and
  tablets are not supported yet.
- **Link.** Open the link we sent you. The first load downloads about 40 MB, then the game also
  works offline.
- **Time.** Each session below takes 30–60 minutes. Do one per sitting if you like.
- **Recording (optional, very helpful).** Record your screen with your voice while you play, and
  think out loud: "I expected X, but Y happened."
- **Controls.** The keys are the ones you already know: Space stages, Shift/Ctrl throttle, Z full,
  X cut, WASD/QE steer, T SAS, R RCS, M map, `.` and `,` warp, F5/F9 quicksave and quickload.
  Shift+F1 shows every key. Esc opens the pause menu.
- **Saves.** Saves live in your browser. Clearing browser data deletes them.

## Session 1: the first hour (Career)

1. Main menu → **New Game** → **Career** → Standard scale → **Start**.
2. At the Space Center, follow the **Next goal** card. Accept a few contracts in **Mission Control**.
3. Build your own first rocket in the **Integration Hall** from the starting parts. Don't use a stock
   design for this one.
4. Fly it, do some science, come home and recover it. Then try for orbit.
5. Keep going as long as you're having fun, but stop after an hour.

**Watch for:** what you expected to work and didn't, any part of the editor that fought you, and
whether you understood why a flight ended the way it did.

## Session 2: the Moon (Sandbox)

1. Main menu → **New Game** → **Sandbox** → **Start**.
2. **Launch Pad** → pick the stock **Selene Lunar Lander** (or build your own).
3. Get to orbit. The **Flight assist** button on the top-right toolbar can fly parts for you, but
   try some yourself.
4. Open the map (M) → **Maneuver planner** → **Transfer** → **Moon**. Pick a departure, fly the
   burn, capture, land, plant a flag on EVA (O to go outside, F to plant), and come home.

**Watch for:** where the planner helped or confused you, how long the waits felt, how landing on
the Moon looked and felt, and whether re-entry and splashdown worked.

## Session 3: free play

Try whatever you would normally try first in a game like this. Some ideas:

- A spaceplane from the runway (**Planes** tab in the Integration Hall).
- Rendezvous and docking (Training → *Rendezvous & docking*, or Challenges → *Rendezvous Race*).
- A rover on the Moon or Mars.
- A mission from **Challenges** (scored, with medals).
- Somewhere far: Mars, Venus or the moons of Saturn.
